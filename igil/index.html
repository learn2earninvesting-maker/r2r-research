The trust layer of the diamond trade

What IGI does, how it earns, and why it's hard to displace.

IGI is the world's largest certifier of lab-grown diamonds (LGD) and the second-largest independent diamond certifier overall. It earns a fee on every grading report. Its labs sit inside the factories of the biggest lab-grown growers, so more production flows straight into more certificates.

"It is the IGI certificate that makes the lab-grown diamond a diamond, and it is the same certificate that also protects the value of a natural diamond." — Management

Business model & moat

  • Paid per report: revenue rises with the number of reports; the average fee per report (ARP) rises as the mix shifts to larger loose stones. India alone certified 1.46 crore stones in the 15 months to March 2026.
  • Lab-grown duopoly: IGI holds ~65% of global lab-grown certifications in a two-player market. The top 5–6 growers control 65–70% of production, which limits price wars among IGI's own customers.
  • Natural diamonds: ~50–55% share in India, 15–25% globally; GIA is the main rival. A dedicated team is pushing for share gains.
  • Network: 36 labs and 18 Schools of Gemology across 10 countries; 12 labs inside major growers' plants.

The long runway

  • Global gems & jewellery certification market: USD 550–650M (2023) → USD 900–1,100M by 2028, ~22% a year, led by lab-grown. Natural diamond and studded jewellery segments grow ~8% each.
  • Diamond jewellery penetration: India ~8% vs US ~70%.

Growth drivers

  • Guidance upgraded: from 15% revenue / 20% EBITDA growth to "similar to H1", i.e. mid-20s revenue growth or higher.
  • Lab-grown supply ramp: Indian growers doubling capacity over three years; CVD reactor count up from ~8.2k to ~10k; polished LGD exports more than doubled in one fiscal year (report: 7.8 to 1.53 crore carats).
  • Retail adoption: 1,000+ LGD showrooms in India; over 50% of US bridal diamonds now lab-grown; Europe, Latin America and Australia opening up.
  • AGL gemstones: USD 13.2M acquisition (Feb 2026) added a fifth segment; now 6.3% of mix; expanding to Jaipur, Thailand, Hong Kong.
  • Geographic build-out: Italy launched, Saudi subsidiary approved, Botswana set up (Sep 2026), Dubai Deira Gold Souk lab opened; overseas certification revenue +31% ex-AGL.
  • Operating leverage: profit expected to grow faster than sales; group margin up at least 100 bps by FY27-end.
Watch: India standalone margin dipped 340 bps to 69.5% on deliberate spending (leadership, IPL marketing, commissions). Management expects it to settle around 70%.

Risks and catalysts

What could go wrong, and what could push the stock higher.

Biggest swing factor: lab-grown diamond pricing. Wholesale prices sit at $80–120/carat; any renewed fall flows into fees per report. Management says pricing is stable.

Risks

  • PRICINGLGD price erosion passes through to average fee per report.
  • CONCENTRATIONDealers: "8-10 guys probably will contribute close to 40%, 50% of our revenues". Dealer sales were 98.79% of FY26 sales; names and terms undisclosed.
  • CASHCash conversion gap: cumulative operating cash covered only 64% of cumulative EBITDA over six years to FY26. Yellow flag on receivables (60-day credit to large growers).
  • DEMANDNatural diamond jewellery softness: fell 19% in Q4 FY26, grew just 3% in Q2 FY27 on volatile gold and silver prices.
  • GEOGRAPHYIndia/Surat dependence: 95% of the world's diamonds are cut and polished in India; ~three-quarters of group revenue is India-linked.
  • REPORTING15-month FY26: year extended (Jan 2025–Mar 2026) without quantifying the effect on comparisons.

Catalysts

  • GUIDANCEH2 to match H1: 27% revenue / 34% EBITDA growth; India festive season and US holiday ordering gathering pace.
  • VOLUMELGD jewellery boom: 1,000+ Indian showrooms, >50% US bridal share, retailer margins on LGD 30–40% higher than natural.
  • SHARENatural diamond share gains: +8% in H1 in a flat market; target double-digit growth by year-end.
  • NEWAGL globalisation: coloured-stone certification moving from New York to Jaipur, Thailand, Hong Kong on a tiny base.
  • EXPANSIONNew labs: Italy, Saudi, Botswana, Deira Gold Souk, plus Tier 2/3 India (Lucknow, Raipur).
  • MARGINMargin expansion: group EBITDA margin above 61% and rising; AI-based grading allocation improving throughput.
