The verdict

The whole report in one screen.

Great business momentum, full price. FY26 sales jumped 41% to ₹3,314 Cr and the profit kept from each ₹100 of sales (margin) rose to ₹32.6, then ₹34.2 in Q1 FY27. The stock is in a confirmed uptrend. But at 51.5x earnings (P/E) with only a 1.0% free-cash yield, there is no margin of safety at current prices.

Business: three engines

What Navin makes and who it sells to.

Founded in 1967, Navin Fluorine is one of India's leading specialty fluorochemical makers and the flagship of the Padmanabh Mafatlal Group. Plants: Surat and Dahej (Gujarat), Dewas (MP) and Manchester (UK). Strategy is "3P": Product, Platform, Partnerships.

① High Performance Products — the cash cow

Refrigerant gases (R22, R32, and newer low-climate-impact HFOs under the "Mafron" brand) and inorganic fluorides. India's first refrigerant maker, India's first and only HFO producer, and one of the world's largest makers of BF3.

The Dahej HFO plant supplies Honeywell under a seven-year, $410 million contract.

② Specialty Chemicals — contract-anchored

Building blocks (intermediates) for crop-protection and pharma products. Plants are built for specific molecules and specific customers, e.g. a ₹540 Cr Dahej plant with peak revenue potential of ~₹600 Cr a year.

Management has deliberately shifted the mix from pharma toward agrochemicals and crop science.

③ CDMO — the growth engine

Manufactures molecules on contract for global drug and crop-science innovators (CDMO). Runs India's only high-pressure fluorination plant meeting global drug-quality rules (cGMP), and serves most of the world's top-20 pharma innovators.

A European CDMO major gives multi-year volume visibility.

New theme — AI data-centre cooling. A Chemours partnership to make Opteon™, a liquid that cools servers by immersion (two-phase immersion cooling) for AI data centres. Navin is Chemours' only manufacturing site for it.

  • Cheaper than its own past: 51.5x P/E is well below its 3- and 5-year medians (62.6x / 66.4x), reflecting a sector-wide correction — yet almost exactly at the industry's 52.0x.
  • You're paying for the ramp: with trailing revenue growing 42% and margins still rising, earnings growth could bring the multiple down on its own.
  • P/B 10.3x vs industry 6.0x assumes high returns on capital stay high (ROCE 18.9% in FY26, up from 10.9% in FY24).
No margin of safety. A PEG of 2.10 (price paid relative to growth) and a 1.0% free-cash yield mean the price already assumes execution goes well.

Risks

Credit profile strong — but read the fine print.

Rating upgraded. In August 2026 CARE upgraded long-term facilities from AA to AA+ (Stable); subsidiary NFASL to AA+ (CE) and AA (unsupported). Caps a decade of consistent CARE AA ratings.

Balance sheet

  • Gross debt ₹1,272 Cr; net debt ₹1,175 Cr (down from ₹1,426 Cr in FY25).
  • Debt-to-equity cut to 0.32x from 0.57x.
  • All ~₹1,226 Cr of group borrowings sit in wholly owned subsidiary NFASL, secured on its assets and fully guaranteed by the listed parent.

Key risks

HIGHDebt parked in a subsidiary
Parent guarantees all group debt in NFASL — the same entity receiving parent equity, preference capital and inter-company loans. Parent exposure to NFASL previously exceeded statutory limits (₹2,578 Cr vs ₹1,980 Cr limit, March 2023).
HIGHCustomer concentration deepened
Three customers each above 10% of consolidated revenue in FY26 (one in FY25). Money owed by customers (receivables) up 47% in two years to ₹752 Cr.
MEDGoodwill / impairment judgement
₹87.76 Cr goodwill and the Dewas unit tested together, never written down — flagged by the auditor as a Key Audit Matter every year since FY2019.
MEDRaw material & pricing cycles
Fluorspar prices have risen over time; refrigerant prices depend on quotas and Chinese supply. FY24 saw weak R22 prices from Chinese exports into the Middle East.
MEDRegulatory phase-down
The Kigali Amendment (global HFC phase-down) is a structural headwind for older refrigerants — though it also drives demand for R32/HFO replacements.
LOWGST demand
₹37.94 Cr demand plus interest and equal penalty (March 2024), being challenged.
LOWAudit-trail exception
Three years running, the auditor flagged that accounting software doesn't record changes by certain privileged users.
