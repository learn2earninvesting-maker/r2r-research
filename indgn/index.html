Verdict at a glance

The one-screen summary of the whole report.

Fast-growing, debt-free pharma-marketing specialist going through a deliberate, dated margin dip. The stock already prices in the growth; a re-rating depends on margins recovering and the first $50mn client arriving.

What works

  • Sales growing 30%+ (acquisitions plus gains from AI-led work)
  • No borrowings; net cash ₹237 Cr
  • Strong cash generation: FY26 operating cash ₹651 Cr = 162% of profit
  • Stock in an uptrend (Weinstein Stage 2, all four checks passed)

What to watch

  • Margins fell from 19.5% (FY24) to ~16.5% — recovery must land by Q4 FY27
  • Top 10 clients = ~49% of sales
  • Open tax demands and a US class-action settlement awaiting court approval
  • Valuation leaves little room if the margin plan slips

Bottom line

At 34.3x earnings versus a 32.4x three-year median, the market is already paying for the growth. The case for a higher valuation rests on (1) margins getting back to 19%+ and (2) Tectonic becoming the first $50mn+ account. Technically the stock is in Stage 2 (advancing).

This is an analytical view, not buy/sell advice.

Theme & business segments

What Indegene does and how it earns money.

Indegene is paid from a pharma client's sales and marketing budget (bought by CMOs), not its IT budget (bought by CIOs) — so it is less exposed to IT cost-cutting.

"Any IT vendor sits on the cost side of the budget, bought by the technology organization and measured on efficiency and arbitrage and is therefore exposed to cost cutting. We sit on the other side." — Management

Growth & financial trajectory

Sales, profit, margins, cash and balance sheet over five years.

Sales more than doubled from FY22 to TTM, but profit is flat since FY25 because margins were deliberately squeezed by investment. Management has dated the recovery: 19–20% EBITDA margin by Q4 FY27.

Why margins dipped — and the fix

Operating margin fell from 19.5% (FY24) to ~16.5% (TTM): ~150bps of deliberate spending on sales reach and GenAI, plus wage hikes and acquisition costs.

Management shortened the recovery window from 6–8 quarters to 6, targeting 19–20% EBITDA margin by Q4 FY27 — tied to contracted revenue (a $10mn+ results-priced marketing deal whose Q4 settlement flows mostly to profit).

"we are confident of getting back our margins to the earlier levels going into FY27" — CFO

Cash quality

  • FY26 operating cash flow ₹651 Cr (162% of profit)
  • FY26 free cash flow ₹606 Cr
  • FY22–26: cash from operations ₹2,402 Cr vs reported profit ₹1,760 Cr — profits are real cash

Balance sheet

  • Net cash ₹237 Cr; zero borrowings; debt/equity 0.00
  • Cash & investments ₹1,389.6 Cr (Q2 FY27)
  • Repaid ~₹400 Cr IPO-linked debt in Q1 FY25; debt-free since
  • Past-due assets fell ₹4,105 Cr → ₹2,834 Cr (FY25→FY26)
  • Days to collect payment (DSO): 69, within the guided mid-60s–70 band

Valuation

How expensive the stock is versus its own past and versus IT services.

Slightly above its own 3-year average and well above generic IT. The premium is defensible (pure life-sciences focus, results-based pricing, 30%+ growth vs low single digits for generic IT) — but it leaves little room if the margin recovery slips.

What the street is watching

Kotak's pre-Q2 note flagged the key swing factors: H2 FY27 growth outlook, BioPharm integration, AI uptake, and "the extent of deflation from GenAI" (i.e. how much AI shrinks the work clients pay for).

Ownership & governance

Who owns the company and any red flags in how it's run.

0% promoter holding — there is no family "promoter". Indegene is professionally managed and backed by PE funds and its founder-executives. Zero pledged shares.

Catalysts & risks

What could push the stock up — and what could go wrong.

Catalysts

Patent-cliff outsourcingTop-20 pharma growing 10–12% YoY with a "very healthy and robust" launch pipeline; clients consolidating content work into AI-led partners.
Tectonic scalingContracted run-rate above $2mn/quarter at the largest client; management hopes it becomes the first $50mn+ account.
Results-based deal settlementThe $10mn+ annual-value marketing deal peaks in Q4 FY27 with costs already spent — a direct margin boost.
Clinical platform$10mn annual-value clinical deal annualising in Q3 FY27; Emerging Biotech revenue +47.7% YoY on a $20mn total-value mandate.
Profit tailwindsAcquisition amortisation drops ₹5 Cr/quarter from Q3 FY27, plus better yield on cash: ~90–95bps boost to pre-tax margin.

Risks

Client concentrationTop 10 clients = 49–51% of revenue; top-20 pharma are ~60% of the addressable market.
Margin executionThe 19–20% target depends on the deal settlement landing on time. Offshore mix actually fell (83.7% → 82.1%) — a past guidance miss.
Tax & legal overhangs₹111.4 Cr transfer-pricing adjustment (no provision; dispute route filed), ₹43.7 Cr income-tax demand (AY 2023-24), ₹20.3 Cr provisioned for a US TCPA class-action settlement awaiting court approval.
Goodwill qualityGoodwill more than doubled to ₹137.6 Cr; impairment tests assume 10% growth vs actual 27% segment growth.
Long-term AI moatManagement: domain expertise defensible for 3–5 years, but "the jury is still out" beyond that.
Currency97%+ of revenue from US/Europe. FY26 EBITDA took a ₹24.1 Cr hedge mark-to-market hit; new hedge accounting removes this from December 2026.
